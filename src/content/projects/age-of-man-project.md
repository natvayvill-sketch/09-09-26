The rise of humanity began to push the magical to the outskirts of society. The exception to this were the empire of Suyei and its sister empire, Evelan. 

The Priestess of Luan were the highest form of the divine on the mortal plane, and were chosen from the royal line. They were more often than not the eldest princess- a pattern repeated for so long that the appointment of Esmeray, the youngest princess, brings unease to the empire. The people whispered that it was a sign of disfavor from the Asmani of the Moon. 

They did not see a child, only a misshapen weapon.
